# Knowledge Village v8 — REAL learning loop integrated with village

## Goal
Owner has approved v6-speed responsive storybook village, bridge and forest clearing. Advance from fake "tap Study => free resources" to a genuinely educational, child-friendly learning-to-village gameplay loop. Child target: Japanese elementary grade 2; central pain point: distinguishing addition and subtraction in written word problems. Tablet/mobile-friendly, Japanese-first.

## Priority / acceptance (MUST)
1. Replace free-reward study buttons with actual interactive **math word problems**. Each task poses a coherent grade-1/2 arithmetic story, with numerical inputs or large answer choices (at least 3 choices, plausible distractors), confirm/retry and optional hints. Include addition, subtraction, comparison, change, remaining, total, word order vs operation. Never reveal answer until appropriate hint/retry; no dead ends. At least 25 hand-authored validated question templates with randomized bounded positive integers and uniquely correct answers; readable Japanese, no ambiguous results, <=100 numbers suitable for grade 2. Code should be tested logically for answer correctness.
2. **Rewards ONLY for correctly solved tasks**, not a button press. Connect earned wood and development points to the existing actual economy, land expansion and building. Difficulty adjusted rewards: full reward without hint, reduced but still encouraging with hint, repeat wrong answers not rewarded repeatedly. Prevent double claim/reload button spam and easy bypass through restarting. Show clear earned reward and warm, informative feedback. No punishment or demotivating text.
3. Add **true progress tracking** to separate versioned, namespaced localStorage just for storybook demo (do not touch primary app storage): counts, recent answers, addition/subtraction mastery by topic, success and hint rates. Reflect in small parent-visible progress summary, not just a raw aggregate counter. Use safe storage migration; do NOT wipe existing village builds, resources or current progress in demo.
4. Provide modest real **Japanese** and **English** interactions instead of free resources: e.g., 10 curated kana/vocabulary reading comprehension questions, 10 basic English word-picture matching/translation tasks with simple multiple-choice. Reward books/globe and progression ONLY after correct answers; clearly mark no speech recognition. If time or scope tight, complete math robustly before extras and label Japanese/English as future features rather than fake free clicks.
5. Keep learning tasks 1 question at a time with easy navigation, no confusing modal over learning sheet. Clear large tap targets >=44px, readable layout for 360–390px mobile and Fire tablet; answer/retry/help states never overlap; family-friendly micro feedback. Add "next question", subject choices and progress meter, do not overwhelm child with diagnostics.
6. Integrate unlocking building types with real verified solved counts per subject, not old button-press skill counts. Existing progress compatibility must be handled without granting new mastery falsely. Ensure arithmetic and economy remain predictable and non-exploitable.
7. **PERFORMANCE REGRESSION BLOCKER**: preserve v6/v7 cached raster scene; absolutely do NOT introduce per-frame tree rendering, expensive learning canvas animations or background fetches. Main app untouched; water flow remains unresolved and is out of scope.
8. No login, no network, no account, no personal child names or data sent anywhere; all progress local to device, transparent to parents. Add optional export/reset of **learning stats only** if safe, but don't include intrusive features.
9. Scope strictly storybook-demo/index.html, style.css, app.js, README.md; retain all established UI, builds, bridge, expansion. Add manual QA checklists, desktop node syntax and sample tests for question generation and reward guards in README.

Note: This is a functional local standalone prototype, NOT yet integrated with main learning app or school curriculum. Do not claim parent dashboard sync or actual adaptive mastery without proof.
